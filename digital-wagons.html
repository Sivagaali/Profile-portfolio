Business challenge

Operational teams need confidence that device telemetry arrives on time and device states are visible.

My contribution

Monitored Grafana dashboards covering IoT data availability, ingestion latency, failures and Sentinel/Beacon status. Investigated unusual signals and supported pipeline incident triage. Gained practical exposure to Azure Databricks and PySpark within the Digital Wagons project.

Databricks & PySpark exposure

Practical exposure to Databricks and PySpark in the project context, alongside pipeline monitoring and data issue investigation. This describes project exposure rather than ownership of the entire Spark platform.

How I approached it

Reviewed availability and latency trends, correlated exceptions with pipeline executions, and escalated actionable findings to relevant teams.

A repeatable monitoring and investigation approach supporting quicker identification of potential data-flow issues. No unverified service-level improvement is claimed.

Public case study: Details are summarised and anonymised. Architecture is conceptual; internal endpoints, sensitive data and proprietary code are excluded. Results are described conservatively.
