Overtime Reporting — Duplicate & Leave Data Quality

← All projects

Business challenge

Roster merges created duplicated matches when unfilled shifts shared blank or zero employee identifiers; reporting totals became misleading.

My contribution

Investigated join cardinality, refined lookup filtering for missing employee names, and validated shift, overtime and leave totals across reporting dimensions.

How I approached it

Reproduced the duplication, examined merge keys and lookup records, applied a targeted Power Query filter, and reconciled results with SQL by plan unit, fortnight and roster stage.

Engineering considerations

  • Null/blank employee names

Outcome & learning

Reduced duplicate-match risk in the lookup and established a practical method for validating downstream calculations; exact business impacts are not published.

Public case study: Details are summarised and anonymised. Architecture is conceptual; internal endpoints, sensitive data and proprietary code are excluded. Results are described conservatively.
